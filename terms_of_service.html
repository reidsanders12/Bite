Reporting content

Every Meal Feed post has a Report option, including a dedicated category for copyright/trademark issues (e.g. someone reposting a restaurant's own photography as theirs) and for harassment or pro-eating-disorder content. You can also Block any user to stop seeing their posts. Reports go to Bite!'s moderation queue for human review; we don't auto-remove content based on a report alone.

They're a starting point for you to review and correct, not medical or nutrition advice -- talk to a qualified professional for guidance specific to your health.
