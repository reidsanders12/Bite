Bite! Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026 · Applies to the Bite! mobile and web app.

Bite! ("we", "us") is a calorie and macro tracking app. This page explains what data Bite! collects, why, and how you can delete it.

What we collect

  • Account info: name, email, password (handled by Supabase Auth -- we never see your raw password), and age (self-reported at signup, used only to enforce the age requirements described under Children below).
  • Biometrics you enter during onboarding: age, gender, weight, height, activity level, and goal -- used only to calculate your calorie/macro targets.
  • Food, workout, and weight logs you create -- meal descriptions, macros, workout descriptions, and weight entries.
  • Photos you submit for analysis (Snap & Log) -- sent to Google Gemini for a one-time macro estimate; we don't separately store the photo itself unless you choose to post it (see below).
  • Meal Feed posts you choose to create -- a photo and optional caption, shared either publicly (visible to any signed-in user) or to one Friend Circle you belong to (visible only to that circle's members).
  • Friend Circle membership and check-in status -- a per-day yes/no against a goal, visible to other members of that circle. Your food logs are never exposed this way.
  • Likes on Meal Feed posts -- recorded, but never attributed to you publicly; only the total count is ever shown to anyone, including other members of a circle.
  • AI Coach chat messages -- kept only in memory on your device for the current session; not stored on our servers.
  • Apple Health / Health Connect activity data (optional, Profile → Connect Health App) -- if you connect it, Bite! reads your steps, walking/running distance, flights climbed, and active/total calories burned for the current day to display in your profile. This is read-only: Bite! never writes anything back to Apple Health or Health Connect, and this data isn't sent to our servers or stored beyond the current app session.

How we use it

To calculate your targets, log and display your history, generate AI-estimated macro/calorie breakdowns, and power the optional social features (Friend Circles, Meal Feed) you choose to use. We don't sell your data, and we don't use it for advertising outside the app's own sponsor promo slot (which is unrelated to your personal data -- sponsors are shown at random, not targeted).

Who we share it with

  • Supabase -- our database, authentication, and file storage provider.
  • Google Gemini -- processes photos/text you submit for AI analysis (Snap & Log, Text Log, AI Coach, workout estimates). Requests go through our own server-side proxy, which never forwards your account email or password.
  • USDA FoodData Central / Open Food Facts -- public nutrition databases we query directly for barcode/ingredient lookups; no personal data is sent to them.

We don't sell or rent your personal data to anyone.

Public and shared content

Meal Feed posts you mark Public are visible to any signed-in Bite! user. Posts you share to a Friend Circle are visible only to that circle's members. You can delete any of your own posts at any time. If you see something that shouldn't be there, every post has a Report option (with categories including harassment, pro-eating-disorder content, and copyright/trademark issues), and you can Block any user to stop seeing their posts. Captions are also screened automatically for known eating-disorder-adjacent language; matches are routed to human review, never auto-deleted. Calorie/macro numbers you optionally attach to a post are only ever shown back to you on your own post -- never to other users, so they're never a comparison point between users.

Data retention and deletion

You can permanently delete your account and everything in it -- food logs, workout history, weight history, meal posts and photos, macro goals, and circle data -- at any time from Profile → Delete Account. If you'd rather not open the app, see how to request deletion by email instead.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Our database uses row-level security so your food logs, workout logs, and weight logs are only ever readable by you -- not by other users, and not even by us through the normal app interface.

Children

Creating a Bite! account requires you to be at least 13 -- age is collected (self-reported) at signup and accounts below that age are refused. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. The Meal Feed and other social features additionally require you to be at least 18; accounts between 13 and 17 can use Bite!'s core tracking features, but the social feed stays hidden for that age range.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page from time to time; continued use of Bite! after a change means you accept the update.
