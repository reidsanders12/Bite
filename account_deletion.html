Delete Your Bite Account

You don't need the app installed to request this.

Option 1: In the app (fastest)

Open Bite → sign in → Profile → Delete Account. This is instant and permanently removes your account and everything in it: food logs, workout history, weight history, meal posts and photos, macro goals, and circle data.

Option 2: By email

If you don't have the app installed, or can't sign in, email us from the address on your account:

Subject: Delete my Bite account

To: you@example.com [REPLACE WITH YOUR SUPPORT EMAIL]

We'll verify the request and delete your account and all associated data within 30 days, and confirm by email once it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (email, password, profile)
  • All food, workout, and weight logs
  • All Meal Feed posts, photos, and likes
  • Macro goals and onboarding biometrics
  • Circles you created (removed for every member); your membership in circles you joined
